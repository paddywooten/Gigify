How it works

From gig to paid, in three steps

No sketchy DMs, no chasing invoices. The money waits safely until the job is done.

1

Post a gig or get discovered

Organizers publish a gig with date, venue and budget. Musicians browse the directory, build a portfolio with audio samples, and apply.

2

Book & fund escrow

The musician is booked, a contract is generated automatically and both sides sign in-app. The client funds escrow — the money sits tight until gig day.

3

Perform & get paid

The organizer confirms the gig is complete and funds release: the musician keeps 85%, Gigify takes its transparent 15%. Everyone's notified by email.

FAQ

Questions, answered straight

How does the escrow actually work?

When a booking is accepted, the client funds the full gig amount into escrow. The money is held securely — not paid out early — until the organizer confirms the gig is complete, at which point the musician is paid out (minus the 15% platform fee). If something goes wrong, either party can raise a dispute and an admin resolves it with a full audit trail.

What does Gigify cost?

Nothing to join, post, browse or apply. A flat 15% platform fee is deducted only when a gig completes — musicians see exactly what they'll keep before accepting, and there are no subscriptions or hidden charges.

Are the contracts legit?

Yes — a gig agreement is generated automatically the moment escrow is funded, snapshotting the gig, date, venue and fee split. It's hashed (SHA-256) so any later edit would be obvious, and each typed-name signature records who signed, when, from which IP and browser. A print-friendly copy is available to both parties.

Which payment providers do you support?

Stripe and Paystack are both integrated behind one payment layer, with signed webhooks and idempotent settlement. Which one you use is a deployment setting — card-entry UI activates as soon as provider keys are configured.
